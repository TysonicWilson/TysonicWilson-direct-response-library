## 16. Reusable Principles

### Principle

**Observation:** The letter discloses the full cost, time commitment, and physical risk of the offer in its opening lines, before any persuasive benefit copy appears.
**Likely Reason:** For a high-ticket, unusual offer aimed at a sophisticated, unaware audience, immediate transparency about the "hard facts" builds trust and self-selects genuinely qualified, motivated prospects rather than wasting persuasive effort on readers who will object once the price is finally revealed.
**General Rule:** When selling a high-cost, unusual offer to an audience capable of affording it, consider leading with full cost and commitment disclosure, then immediately reframe that disclosure as the price of an exclusive privilege.
**Use When:** The offer's genuine value/prestige can credibly outweigh the disclosed cost and effort once properly framed, and concealment would likely backfire with a sophisticated audience.
**Do Not Blindly Use When:** The audience is price-sensitive or unaware of the category, where leading with a large price before any value-building could cause immediate rejection before the reframe has a chance to land.
