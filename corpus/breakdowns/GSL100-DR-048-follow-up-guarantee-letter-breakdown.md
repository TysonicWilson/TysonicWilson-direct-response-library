The literal subject is a short, single-purpose follow-up letter sent after an initial product sales letter. ## 16. Reusable Principles

### Principle

**Observation:** The letter isolates a single persuasive element (the guarantee) from a longer original sales letter and gives it a dedicated, standalone follow-up communication entirely to itself.
**Likely Reason:** A reader who did not respond to an initial full sales letter may be hesitating on one specific dimension (often risk) rather than needing the entire argument repeated; isolating and re-emphasizing that one element in a short, focused follow-up can address the actual remaining objection more efficiently than resending the full pitch.
**General Rule:** When following up with non-responders to an initial sales letter, consider isolating the single most likely remaining objection (commonly risk/guarantee, price, or a key benefit) and dedicating a short, focused follow-up communication to that element alone, rather than resending the complete original argument.

**Do Not Blindly Use When:** The reason for non-response is unclear or likely due to a lack of basic product awareness rather than a specific objection, in which case a narrow single-topic follow-up would fail to address the reader's actual barrier to conversion.
