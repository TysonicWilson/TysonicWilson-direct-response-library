**Likely Reason:** In a relationship-building (rather than transactional) touch, leading with genuine personal content rather than business language signals that the communication is not "just another sales follow-up," increasing the odds the recipient reads it fully and forms a positive emotional association with the sender, independent of any immediate business ask.
**General Rule:** When the goal of a communication is long-term relationship-warming rather than an immediate transaction, consider structuring the piece so that personal, non-commercial content dominates, with any business reference kept brief, soft, and clearly secondary to the human content.
