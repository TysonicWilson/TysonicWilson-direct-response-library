## 16. Reusable Principles

### Principle

**Observation:** The letter opens by explicitly disqualifying the most likely self-exclusion objection ("You don't have to be a professional...") before making any offer.
**Likely Reason:** Cold prospects often silently self-exclude from offers they assume are "not for them"; naming and dismissing that assumption in the first line keeps a wider set of readers engaged through the rest of the letter.
**General Rule:** When an offer could plausibly be misjudged as being for a narrower audience than it actually is, state the inclusive scope explicitly in the opening line, before any other persuasive content.
**Use When:** The offer's target audience is genuinely broader than its subject matter might suggest at first glance.
**Do Not Blindly Use When:** The offer genuinely is narrow/niche — falsely broadening perceived audience fit can attract poor-fit responders and increase cancellations/refunds.
**Example From This Promotion:** "You don't have to be a professional to be interested in -- even fascinated by -- this hypothetical problem... All it takes is a normal amount of curiosity."

### Principle

**Observation:** The letter's P.S. explicitly invites the reader to signal a "no" decision using the same easy response device used for "yes," rather than leaving non-response as the only decline path.
**Likely Reason:** An explicit, low-effort decline mechanism can increase overall response tracking accuracy and may preserve goodwill/brand perception even among non-buyers, potentially aiding future remailing efforts.
**General Rule:** When using a distinctive physical or interactive response device, consider extending it to cover a graceful "no" response as well as "yes," rather than assuming silence is an acceptable decline signal.
**Use When:** The response mechanism is inexpensive and low-friction enough that a formal decline path adds negligible cost.
**Do Not Blindly Use When:** Encouraging active declines could meaningfully suppress response rates that would otherwise be captured through simple non-response.
**Example From This Promotion:** "P.S. Even if your decision is not to try Psychology Today, will you tell us by putting the token in the 'no' slot? Thank you."
